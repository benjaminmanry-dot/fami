# Composition Templates

Use these master templates for professional coordinate quilting designs. They are original composition structures, not copies of any the textile designer pattern.

## Heirloom Pearl Diamond Medallion

Use for: focal blocks, center medallions, square custom areas.

Structure:

- Outer square frame.
- Inner square mat.
- Central pearl-edged diamond.
- Focal rosette or flower at center.
- Four directional motifs at diamond points.
- Corner support motifs in the surrounding square.
- Optional sashing-like pearl band above and below.

Best motifs:

- focal-flower-rosette
- pearl-chain
- flower-echo
- echoed-heart
- flourish-scroll
- leaf-sprig

Reject if:

- Diamond is present but does not frame a focal motif.
- Corner motifs are unrelated to the center.
- Pearl spacing varies visibly.

## Layered Scroll Border With Corner Turns

Use for: outer borders, wide borders, border/corner coordinate sets.

Structure:

- Outer containment rail.
- Inner containment rail.
- Primary scroll or vine spine.
- Repeating focal accent every cell.
- Pearl or echo lane.
- Corner rosette or scroll turn.

Best motifs:

- layered-border-frame
- pearl-chain
- flourish-scroll
- leaf-sprig
- echoed-heart
- sakura-rosette fragment

Reject if:

- Border is a cropped block.
- Corners are not designed.
- Connector travel is naked.

## Quiet Sashing Chain

Use for: sashing, narrow borders, support strips.

Structure:

- Single rail or soft vine.
- Small alternating motifs.
- Optional dot/pearl accents.
- Same-plane start and end points for chaining.

Best motifs:

- leaf-sprig
- small echoed-heart
- small pearl-chain
- tiny scroll

Reject if:

- Sashing competes with focal blocks.
- Repeats are too dense for the strip.
- Motifs are larger than the rail can support.

## Feathered Setting Triangle

Use for: setting triangles, corner triangles, triangular background spaces.

Structure:

- Broad-edge entry.
- Diagonal or radial feather/leaf spine.
- Motifs taper toward the point.
- Optional clamshell or pearl fill along the broad edge.

Best motifs:

- feather-plume
- leaf-sprig
- clamshell-arc
- small flower echo
- small pearl-chain

Reject if:

- It looks like a square block squeezed into a triangle.
- The point contains awkward partial motifs.
- Density changes abruptly from adjacent blocks.

## Rosette Corner Turn

Use for: corner blocks, border corners, custom transition areas.

Structure:

- Entry from one border axis.
- Rosette, flower, or scroll cluster at the turn.
- Pearl or leaf transition.
- Exit on adjacent border axis.

Best motifs:

- sakura-rosette
- pearl-chain
- leaf-sprig
- flourish-scroll
- echoed-heart

Reject if:

- The turn is just a miter line.
- The corner motif looks pasted onto border repeats.

## Alternate Floral Block

Use for: alternate blocks or support blocks.

Structure:

- Square or soft scallop frame.
- Smaller focal motif.
- Two or four corner accents.
- Echo halo or pearl mat.
- Reduced motif mix compared with the focal block.

Best motifs:

- sakura-rosette
- flower-echo
- leaf-sprig
- pearl-chain
- echoed-heart

Reject if:

- It contains the full focal motif library.
- It lacks a center/frame/corner relationship.

## Whole-Quilt Coordinate Layout

Use for: complete coordinate preview across borders, blocks, triangles, and focal areas.

Structure:

- Outer border with designed corners.
- Inner border or sashing band.
- Focal medallion or focal block.
- Supporting blocks with reduced motif mix.
- Triangles with diagonal/tapering logic.
- Similar density from geography to geography.

Reject if:

- Pieces feel unrelated.
- One area is much denser than the others.
- Motifs are repeated everywhere without omission.
