# Platform Tone

## Reddit

Use short paragraphs, one detail from the post, plain paid disclosure, and no formal signoff. Prefer a useful public reply when rules permit it. Do not move to DM unless the post or rules invite it.

## Structured forums

Mirror the post's detail with two or three short paragraphs. Address schedule, style, or group needs directly. Use one relevant link only after paid openness and link permission are clear.

D&D Beyond signals remain eligible for research and qualification, but its site rules make outreach `manual_only`. Do not draft, rewrite, suggest, edit, or provide talking points for text intended for that forum; Ben writes independently.

## Admin or moderator messages

Ask one rule question directly. State the professional-DM connection, promise no unsolicited bulk contact, and do not pitch the service.

## Discord

Follow server norms and use admin-approved or opt-in channels. Keep messages brief. Legitimate server access is not permission to DM members.

## Default edit

Delete any opening that starts with Ben instead of the prospect, replace abstract fit with one observed detail, cut sentences that exist only to sound professional, keep one question, preserve paid disclosure, and preserve the easy opt-out.
