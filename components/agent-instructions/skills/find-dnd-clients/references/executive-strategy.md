# Acquisition Strategy and Research Method

## Business outcome

Optimize for a retained paid seat, not raw leads or booked calls. The useful leading indicator is a qualified private conversation with Ben through Discord, text, phone, the 20Fates form, or a platform reply that the source permits.

The funnel is:

`current public need -> permissioned contact -> trust via 20Fates -> private conversation with Ben -> Session Zero when appropriate -> paid seat -> retained four weeks`

Do not force every person through every step. A high-intent adult—or a legal guardian managing contact—who explicitly wants a paid DM may go directly to Ben. An unknown-age signal gets one neutral age-or-guardian question before any pitch, link, offer claim, or owner handoff. Someone whose paid openness is unknown gets one paid-openness question before a link or pitch.

## Evidence discipline

Keep four classes of information separate:

1. **Observed:** exact public words, URL, post time, observation time, source rule, and contact route.
2. **Inferred:** likely fit, with confidence and the evidence that supports it.
3. **Generated:** internal summaries and campaign suggestions, plus draft messages only where AI-generated outreach is permitted.
4. **Outcome:** explicit events after approval, such as reply status or a paid seat.

Only observed evidence may determine qualification. Generated text, notes, campaign copy, and outcomes must never improve a prospect's eligibility.

## Research record

For an individual prospect record (not a source-only community inventory):

- source URL and platform/community
- exact query or navigation path used
- posted time and observed time
- concise evidence summary plus a direct quotation when available
- age evidence and `audience_age_state`, including an explicit unknown
- legal-guardian evidence and guardian-only contact route when a minor is involved
- paid-openness evidence or an explicit unknown
- current permission evidence, allowed contact route, and AI-content policy
- schedule, system, style, and player-count evidence when stated
- inferences, confidence, and unknowns in their own fields
- Ben's research/review/conversation minutes as they occur

If a fact was not stated, record it as unknown. Do not turn absence into a favorable inference.

## Research standard

- Prefer fresh first-party posts over profile enrichment or generic community size.
- Prefer a few current high-intent opportunities over a large speculative list.
- Treat each source-specific tactic as a hypothesis until explicit outcomes support it.
- Record misses as carefully as wins. A visually polished draft is not evidence of fit.
- Bound the batch by the requested coverage, expected value and available resources. Ten is not an authority limit. Community discovery returns community evidence; absence of a qualified individual buyer does not make that assignment unsuccessful.
