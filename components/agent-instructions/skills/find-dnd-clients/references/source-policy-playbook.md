# Source Access and Permission Policy

## Structural gate

No opportunity is outreach-ready until the record contains current evidence that commercial participation and the proposed contact route are allowed. Record the AI-content policy separately: a prohibition sets `manual_only` and blocks every AI-generated outreach artifact, but it does not remove an otherwise viable source or prospect from research and qualification.

This gates outreach, not public/legitimately accessible research. A community can be a useful discovery result with an explicitly unresolved rule or access question. Do not demand a buyer's age, exact table match or message approval to finish a community inventory; those belong to later individual qualification. Existing authorized read access needs no new permission ritual.

Use only:

- public content;
- content Ben can legitimately access;
- admin-approved spaces and channels; or
- direct contact routes the prospect intentionally published for this purpose.

Do not scrape member lists, infer private contact details, evade access controls, impersonate a player, or treat account access as outreach permission.

## Rules audit

At the start of each source test, capture:

- source/community name and exact URL;
- observation date and rules URL;
- visibility and legitimate access path;
- exact language governing paid games, promotion, links, and DMs;
- exact language governing AI-generated content, or evidence that the current rules were checked and state no such restriction;
- approved channel, thread, day, or format;
- any source-level age restriction; individual age evidence belongs to a prospect record, not every community audit;
- admin decision, decision-maker, and expiry if one exists; and
- the allowed contact route.

Classify the source as one of:

- `test`: current allowed route is evidenced; this may include `manual_only` sources that require Ben-authored outreach;
- `audit_rules`: access exists but policy is missing, unclear, or expired;
- `request_access`: legitimate access or admin permission is required;
- `blocked`: no legitimate access path is visible; or
- `reject`: commercial contact is prohibited, the source itself is minors-focused, or the source is hostile/fake/inactive. An individual minor opportunity may proceed only when a legal guardian is evidenced and manages the contact route.

Re-check source rules before each sprint and immediately before outreach when the stored policy has expired or the source appears to have changed. A visible paid post is evidence that paid posts exist, not evidence that unsolicited replies, DMs, or AI-written content are allowed.

## Access and admin requests

Draft from `assets/access-request-template.md` or `assets/admin-message-template.md`. Ask for one specific action in one specific channel. Disclose the professional-DM connection and make refusal easy.

Ben must approve the exact request and recipient before it is sent. Never auto-submit a join request, admin message, post, DM, or email.

Store reusable policy facts in `assets/source-rules-memory-template.csv`. Store prospect opportunities separately in `assets/acquisition-tracker-template.csv`; source policy is not prospect evidence.
