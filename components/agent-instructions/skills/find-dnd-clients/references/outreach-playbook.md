# Outreach, Replies, and Owner Handoff

## Choose one route

First check `ai_content_state`. If it is `manual_only` or `unknown`, stop at a research packet. Keep the prospect in the qualified list, but do not produce example language, edits, talking points, or a draft intended for that source; Ben must write independently outside this skill if he chooses to proceed.

Then check `audience_age_state`:

- `adult_confirmed`: continue normally.
- `unknown`: use `verify_age_or_guardian`; the only permitted outreach purpose is confirming that every player is 18+ or that a legal guardian will manage the conversation. Do not include a pitch, link, campaign, price, or owner-contact invitation.
- `guardian_managed_minor`: communicate only with the evidenced legal guardian, using the recorded guardian route. Never address outreach or owner-conversation material to the minor.
- `explicit_minor`: reject.

Use the least presumptive route supported by evidence:

### Clarify paid openness

Use when an adult prospect or legal guardian clearly wants a DM or game but has not indicated whether paid play is acceptable. Ask one short question. Do not include a campaign menu, link, price, or owner-contact push yet.

Example shape: “You mentioned [specific need]. Are paid professional games on the table, or are you looking only for a free community game?”

### Website first

Use when paid openness and permission are established but the prospect still needs trust or context. Respond to one specific need, disclose paid service, and give one relevant 20Fates link. Explain that the site shows the current price, tables, and ways to talk directly with Ben.

### Direct owner conversation

Use for a fresh, permissioned adult prospect—or a legal guardian managing contact—who explicitly wants a paid DM and has a concrete need. Offer the current Discord, text, phone, form, or permitted platform-reply route from the fresh website cache. For a minor player, address and route everything only to the guardian. Let the prospect or guardian choose; do not force a voice call or appointment.

## Draft rules

- Produce one recommended message, not a menu of variants.
- Start with the prospect's stated need.
- Use one concrete fit point and one clean question.
- Say that Ben's games are paid before asking for a commitment.
- Use only price, schedule, seat, scarcity, and contact facts supported by a fresh website cache.
- Make “no” easy and do not manufacture urgency.
- Do not send until Ben approves the exact message and recipient.
- Do not draft or rewrite any content for a source or prospect that prohibits AI-generated content.

## Approval packet

Show Ben:

- prospect/source and URL;
- exact observed need, age state and evidence, and guardian evidence/route when applicable;
- permission evidence and route;
- paid-openness state;
- freshness;
- supported campaign match or “Talk with Ben about fit”;
- one draft only when `ai_content_state` is `permitted`; otherwise show `manual_only` and no language artifact;
- unknowns and risks; and
- the exact next action requiring approval.

## Replies

Answer the question asked before advancing the funnel. If paid openness becomes negative, close politely. If schedule or campaign fit is uncertain, route to a general conversation with Ben rather than inventing a match. Record reply state explicitly; never infer it from notes.

## Conversation-to-seat brief

Before Ben takes over, give him a compact brief:

- what the person wants and why now;
- age/guardian and paid-openness evidence;
- group size, schedule, system, and style;
- the supported campaign/schedule, or why fit remains general;
- material unknowns or objections actually stated;
- source and permission context; and
- the next natural question.

Session Zero comes after the first conversation when it is the right fit. Track invitation, booking, completion, paid seat, and four-week retention as separate events.
