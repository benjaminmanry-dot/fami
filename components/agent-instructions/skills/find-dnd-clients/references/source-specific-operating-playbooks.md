# Source-Specific Working Hypotheses

These are research hypotheses, not proven high-conversion channels. Audit the current rules before every test and let explicit retained-seat outcomes change the priority.

## Reddit and public forums

- Likely signal: a fresh post states group size, schedule, DM need, or willingness to pay.
- Risk: subreddit/forum rules may prohibit paid offers, solicitation, cross-posting, or unsolicited DMs.
- Test: one responsive public reply or permitted DM, whichever the current rules and post invite.
- Tone: short on Reddit; slightly fuller on structured forums.

### Current audited examples — re-check before use

- `r/lfg` (checked 2026-08-12): its public sidebar says paid games, services, and donations may not be solicited or offered. Classify paid acquisition there as `reject`.
- `r/lfgpremium` (checked 2026-08-12): paid play is the community's purpose, but the current listing scan was dominated by GM offers rather than player-buying posts. Treat this as a sourcing observation, not proof of prospect demand.
- `r/DnDLFG` (checked 2026-08-12): paid listings were visible, but the public sidebar did not expressly authorize commercial replies to individual LFG posts. A visible paid listing is not enough permission; require a prospect-invited route or moderator clarification.

## D&D Beyond forums

- Paid-contact rule (guide last edited 2026-04-09): paid DMs may offer games only to posts explicitly open to paid play. When paid openness is unknown, one neutral public question is allowed, but no advertisement may be included until the prospect consents. Unsolicited paid private messages are treated as spam.
- AI-content rule (checked 2026-08-12): D&D Beyond's site rules prohibit content created in whole or in part through AI.
- Structural consequence: keep qualifying D&D Beyond signals and mark them `manual_only`. This skill may collect and classify public evidence, but must not draft, rewrite, suggest, edit, or supply talking points for any post, reply, or private message intended for D&D Beyond; Ben must write it independently.
- Sources: <https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/looking-for-players-groups/15196-looking-for-group-player-please-read-before> and <https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/news-announcements/208663-site-rules-guidelines>.

## Roll20 and other LFG marketplaces

- Likely signal: users are already comparing tables and schedules.
- Risk: marketplace-specific listing, fee, or off-platform-contact rules.
- Test: use only the native route the current platform allows; do not divert users around platform rules.

## Discord communities

- Likely signal: active opt-in LFG channels with current requests; record adult, unknown-age, and guardian-managed evidence without guessing.
- Risk: legitimate server access does not imply permission to DM members.
- Test: admin-approved or rules-designated channel only; use DMs only when explicitly invited.

## Facebook and Meetup groups

- Likely signal: local groups, private parties, workplace events, and organized communities; retain unknown-age signals for verification.
- Risk: gated membership, promotional-day restrictions, and stale posts.
- Test: request access transparently, read the current rules, and use the named promotional route.

## Convention, venue, and organization boards

- Likely signal: a named organizer with a concrete date or event need.
- Risk: vendor approval and commercial terms may be required.
- Test: ask the organizer or venue for permission before pitching individuals.

## Evaluation

For each source, track opportunities, contacts, replies, qualified owner conversations, Session Zeros, paid seats, four-week retention, and Ben's time. Do not call a source scalable from one win or from activity without outcomes.
