# New Campaign And Session One

Use this before an ongoing campaign's first played session. It establishes campaign state and helps Ben choose a direction; it does not reproduce Story Engine's adventure pipeline.

## Intake

Read the user's brief or `templates/campaign_intake.md`. Capture the campaign name/root, game system, runtime, premise, tone, starting situation, known party facts, established NPCs/factions/places, table boundaries, and desired first-night experience.

## Approved Starter Canon

Treat user-provided facts as approved starter canon unless they are marked uncertain, optional, exploratory, or "maybe." Put generated connective tissue in a proposed section or canon docket. Preserve provenance.

## Direction Slate

Unless Ben has already chosen, offer two to four directions. Each should state:

- opening image;
- central pressure;
- likely player action and possible exits;
- likely climax or stopping edge;
- minimum assets;
- why it fits;
- the decision Ben must make.

This is a decision aid, not a hidden module manuscript. Stop after the slate until Ben selects or revises a direction.

## After Selection

- For quick, flexible first-night prep, use the light session plan and compact running-notes templates in this skill.
- For a bounded polished episode, keyed site, dungeon, module, or any explicit Story Engine-quality outline/manuscript/running notes, hand the selected direction to canonical Story Engine.

The handoff supplies exact campaign root, approved entry sources/state, party/system/runtime/table constraints, selected direction, unresolved facts, and intended handback. Story Engine outputs remain proposed until played and later reviewed through the campaign's canon process.
