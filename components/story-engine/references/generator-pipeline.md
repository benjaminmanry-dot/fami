# Adventure production guidance

The outcome is one cold-runnable native table reader from one authoritative manuscript. Develop the adventure in the sequence that makes its central situation, spatial play, and reading experience work. The sections below describe responsibilities, not mandatory sequential phases or separate reports.

## Direction and scope

Read the current brief and approved decisions before proposing work. Standalone adventures live in this repository's `one-shots/<slug>/`; campaign episodes live in their owning campaign's established prep location. Record exact continuity sources and return proposed outcomes without promoting canon.

When Ben has not chosen a direction, explore distinct possibilities in kind, not cosmetic variations; recommend the one with the strongest playable pressure and explain its risk. A concept slate or structure proposal can itself be the commissioned product. When he has already selected or approved the structure, use it. Preserve his structure and final decisions without introducing approval moments for internal drafts, map work, exports, or skill handoffs.

Retrieve important missing facts; choose reversible details. Keep assumptions and genuine deferrals in the existing brief. A field-complete template is not evidence that an adventure works.

## Authoring and spatial design

Read the relevant craft references named by `SKILL.md`. Establish the opening, concrete central pressure, likely routes, consequential encounters, climax, and aftermath. Let text and map design inform one another. Named usable features should have perceptible introductions and physical correspondence. Preserve current entry facts, identities, possessions, time, and NPC state.

Use actual table evidence for encounter calibration; examine action economy, control, recovery, and the load-bearing interaction that might undo the central pressure. Put only the rules and consequences the DM needs in the manuscript. Keep design arithmetic and source analysis in working notes. Read-aloud, NPC moves, room features, checks, treasure, and exits belong at first use. No separate running-notes product.

Respect player departures: furnish still-true situations that invite meaningful play after choices change the fiction. Pacing may compress expendable material but does not erase consequences or force missed checkpoints. A beautiful conceptual outline is incomplete if the DM cannot start and sustain play from it.

## Reader and private drafts

Compile into the owning project's native local 5e.tools runtime using its existing builder where available. Preserve manuscript meaning and exact source/output identities. Link areas, creatures, rules, handouts, and relevant map regions; place maps before dependent keys. Open the real interface and test the user path.

Private production drafts are allowed within the adventure commission. Display DRAFT-ONLY outside the adventure prose, name missing maps/assets and deferred checks in the review record, and do not claim complete readiness. Map Engine's draft-only evidence route is distinct from strict v1.3 review/accepted eligibility. Never rewrite a historical rejected manifest or reuse contaminated pixels to bypass that distinction.

One static map normally suffices. Only Ben's explicit fictional-transformation request creates variant scope. Player, gridded, and GM exports do not create extra fictional states. A PDF or Roll20 checklist is produced only when requested; neither replaces the native reader.

## Verification and completion

The owner checks the actual reader and relevant deterministic invariants first. One independent cold reviewer then examines actual use and relevant claims under `qa-panel.md`, starting without production explanations. Add a specialist only for a named unresolved risk. Preserve findings, fix material defects, and recheck the affected scope; an unchanged domain needs no ceremonial rerun.

Internal verification, Ben's acceptance, and observed table success are distinct. Present the complete reader for his final acceptance with concise evidence, scope, and uncertainty. Preserve an emergency-use or partial-review disposition exactly. If a Ben-set resource/quality stop is reached, preserve recovery and name the actual remaining failure; do not quietly shrink the product. Agent-chosen internal phase budgets may be rebalanced within the whole commission.
