# Independent actual-reader review

Use one capable fresh-context reviewer for the finished reader's relevant claims. The historical filename does not require a panel. The owner performs its own checks and fixes; the reviewer supplies an independent challenge. Add a separate rules or visual specialist only when a specific material uncertainty exceeds this reviewer's capabilities. Record the reason, not a ritual role roster.

## Cold use before explanations

Give the reviewer the actual local reader and its scale/navigation instructions, without the brief, production discussion, prior findings, or intended verdict. The reviewer must be able to inspect the interface and images; a reviewer without that capability cannot certify them. Preserve its first findings before supplying source context.

Ask the reviewer to run the opening and likely route as a paid-table DM seeing it for the first time. Can it establish the current situation, why engagement matters, the first meaningful decision, and ways onward? Follow maps and links, test a keyed interaction, a consequential encounter, the climax, and the ending. Name the play moment where an essential fact, route, rule, or usable physical description is missing. Do not penalize ordinary improvisation for lacking a prewritten branch.

Choose one plausible unscripted departure and identify a still-true evocative situation that supports meaningful player activity while the DM regains bearings. Preserve changed fiction; do not invent a rescue branch or force the planned sequence.

For maps, inspect practical play scale and full resolution. Trace routes, boundaries, elevations, and recognizable room use before reading semantic keys. Then compare box text, named features, map positions, and actual mechanics. Construction legibility and beauty both matter; neither compensates for the other. Use Map Engine's appropriate anchor comparison when assessing house style. One reviewer may record these distinct claims in the same report.

## Source and rules comparison

After the cold findings are saved, give the reviewer the manuscript, brief, Ben-grade taste reference, mechanical floor, and relevant map evidence. Check continuity of names, possessions, locations, time, clues, routes, creature state, mechanical values, campaign entry facts, and endings. Audit the important rules: exact creature assignments, custom abilities, checks with consequences, clocks, rewards, encounter interactions, and load-bearing exceptions. Respect Ben's observed table calibration.

Judge specificity, emotional pressure, usable strangeness, clear first-use prose, and economy against `ben-grade-gate.md`, with quoted manuscript evidence for positive quality claims. Its recorded taste is preserved; fixed pass sequences and panel headcount are superseded by this procedure. A generic but competent adventure remains at most usable.

## Evidence and disposition

Record exact artifact identities, reviewer identity/capability, supplied context, what was actually exercised, material findings with locations and corrections, and uncertainty. `templates/one_shot_quality_review.md` is an adaptable record. No mandatory row count or separate report per claim.

The actual reader check covers reading order, complete relevant maps before first use, resolved links, source preservation, and no broken rendering, placeholders, missing assets, or production leakage. When a PDF was requested, inspect every page for layout and source fidelity. A parse/build/hash check proves only its own property.

A DRAFT-ONLY review may proceed with missing maps or incomplete checks. State them explicitly; it cannot certify the omitted claims or complete-reader readiness. Complete-reader QA requires no unresolved session-breaking or scene-breaking defect, no critical mechanical issue, a strong-or-better evidenced craft judgment, and a functioning actual interface with its required assets. Ben then decides final acceptance.

Preserve the first report unchanged. Correct the material defect and recheck the affected boundary. Repeat wider work only when a change invalidates the earlier evidence. Stop when no material problem remains; do not add criticism to justify another pass. Never relabel a failure, Ben's limited emergency-use disposition, or an unperformed check as a quality pass.
