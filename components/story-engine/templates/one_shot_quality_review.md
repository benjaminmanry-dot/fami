# Adventure Quality Review

Use for the independent actual-artifact review in `references/qa-panel.md`. The owner may assemble factual build/check evidence; attribute independent judgments to the reviewer. Adapt the sections to the product rather than completing irrelevant rows.

## Actual-reader cold run

Record the first findings before source/brief comparison: opening and engagement, likely traversal, first-use rules and maps, a consequential encounter, climax, ending, and one plausible unscripted departure with a still-true evocative situation. Name any failure by the actual play moment and reader location.

## Relevant evidence

- Craft and Ben-grade taste: rating with quoted manuscript proof for each claim made.
- Rules and encounter calibration: checks performed, important exceptions, supplied table evidence.
- Map: practical/full-resolution route and feature correspondence, construction legibility, beauty, and known limits.
- Continuity: source/brief/reader agreement and any quoted contradictions.
- Interface/export: paths and links exercised, required assets, requested PDF inspection only.

## Material findings and correction

For each finding: artifact/location, observed problem, effect on play, needed correction, and targeted recheck. Preserve the original independent findings. Do not fill this section with hypothetical defects or ordinary DM improvisation.

## Disposition

State what is verified, what remains uncertain, and whether the complete reader is ready for Ben's final decision. A draft review does not certify omitted checks. Internal QA, usable-tonight permission, Ben's professional acceptance, and live integration authority remain separate.
