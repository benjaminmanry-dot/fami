# 20Fates Map Engine

Produce a convincing place the players can act in: complete inhabited rooms or coherent outdoor spaces joined by real routes, with beautiful vivid retro pop-art finish and readable tactical geometry. The map must support the adventure's box text, named usable features, and actual mechanics. Neither paperwork nor a visually impressive isolated prop substitutes for the whole place.

## Begin from the commissioned outcome

Retrieve the approved structure, source manuscript, current map direction, audience, scale, routes, elevations, starts, exits, hazards, important interactables, and must-preserve geometry. Preserve Ben's decisions without asking again. A standalone map commission does not need an invented Story Engine gate. Ordinary production uses one static fictional map; only Ben's explicit request adds fictional variants. Player/grid/GM derivatives remain the same fiction.

Read `references/approved-contract.md` for current boundaries and `references/house-style-profile.v1.json` when choosing the medium and relevant comparison anchors. Use `references/workflow.md` for practical construction guidance. Load audit/graph schemas and templates only when modular construction or strict legacy release packaging actually calls for them. They are useful engineering tools, not universal creative prerequisites.

## Author the place

Work from connected layout through rooms and activity groups to recognizable objects and selective accents. Give inhabited places ordinary furnishings, work space, storage, access, outputs, and traces of occupants. A dominant object is optional; if present, the surrounding space explains its use. Purposeful emptiness and monumental space need no furnishing quota.

Make walls, thresholds, stairs, retaining edges, ramps, platforms, and boundaries physically plausible and readable. Match projection, scale, lighting, material, contact shadows, and occlusion. A route must actually connect; labels cannot rescue false stairs, sealed entrances, floating architecture, or disconnected rooms. Furnishings can stand together without invented architectural sockets.

Choose capable means for the actual scene: authored raster, drawing, clean generated assets, integrated construction, or a combination. Reuse coherent assets when they fit; create necessary missing assets within the commission. Do not force a sparse catalogue, stack incompatible finished props, build a general editor, or shrink the creative outcome to fit convenient code. Finish and construction can develop together; use a sketch or cheap proof when it resolves a real costly uncertainty, not as a mandatory pre-finish ceremony.

## Make it beautiful and inspect it

Set emotional intent, governing composition, supporting forms, quiet circulation, palette/value opposition, and physical mark language. Ben's target is vivid retro pop-art with authored material richness: broad readable masses, selective ink and texture, purposeful irregularity, unequal contrast and detail. Avoid generic floor-plan primitiveness, sticker rooms, equal-detail collage, synthetic shine, uniform distress, and decorative noise. Study appropriate anchors at comparable practical scale; an old accepted sparse map is not a ceiling for a richly furnished site.

Inspect the whole map at play scale and important regions at native resolution. Verify routes, clearances, elevations, keyed features, object functions, physical joins, projection, shadows, and repeated defects. Construction legibility and beauty are separate quality claims; both must hold in the actual final artifact. Source hashes establish identity, not either claim.

Use one capable independent actual-artifact cold reviewer, which may also own the adventure cold run. Follow `references/cold-composition-review.md`: first inspect the unlabeled map and scale, then compare the source/key and appropriate anchors. The same report can cover all relevant claims. Add another specialist only for a named material risk outside that reviewer's competence. Preserve findings; correct and recheck affected scope within Ben's whole-job budget and explicit stops. Agent-selected procedural subcaps may be reconsidered within that budget. Do not demand new human approvals for routine internal steps.

## Identity, drafts, and acceptance

Preserve original sources, source permissions, candidate identities, and failed reports. Never use rejected, contaminated, or inspection-only pixels as production lineage. Restart from clean sources and written decisions when contamination recurs. External benchmark rights and role limits remain unchanged.

Read `references/release-manifest-contract.md` when preparing a private reader or handoff. A new clearly labeled **DRAFT-ONLY** package can be inspected in an authorized private local reader before final quality checks. Its separate draft evidence records exact artifacts, lineage, known limits, and unperformed checks; it cannot satisfy accepted/live release flags. New complete maps use v1.4: exact artifacts/sources, one independent final review with concrete evidence, and Ben's exact decision for accepted status. No legacy phase chain is needed. Do not create fake PASS records, rewrite a rejected record, or relabel rejected art. Strict v1.3 accepted and review records keep their existing validator semantics.

Record the completed product, actual checks, uncertainty, resource use, and Ben's exact disposition in the owning project. Internal verification, usable-tonight permission, and professional-quality acceptance remain distinct. No map production is authorized by skill maintenance alone. Do not publish, deploy, spend, install, change canon, contact people, or enter live VTT without the applicable explicit authority. A stopped artifact-specific attempt remains stopped unless Ben renewed it.
