# Testimonial Request Message

Use this as a starting point. Personalize it before sending.

```text
Hi [name],

I am starting to collect a few real player quotes for 20Fates so future players can get a better sense of what the tables are actually like.

Would you be open to sharing a short testimonial about your experience playing with us?

No pressure at all, and it is completely fine to say no. If you do send something, I will not publish it without checking the final wording with you first. You can also choose how you want to be credited, including first name, first name + last initial, initials, campaign context, or anonymous.

If it helps, you can answer any one of these:

- What made you keep coming back?
- What would you tell someone nervous about joining?
- What makes this table different from other D&D games you have played?
- What kind of player would enjoy this?
- What is one campaign moment or feeling that stuck with you?
- How has the table/community affected your week or friendships?

Short is completely fine. A few honest sentences are more useful than something polished.

Thanks either way,
Ben
```
