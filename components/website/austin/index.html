What is 20Fates?

A professional D&D campaign studio for long-term players.

20Fates is a professional D&D campaign studio run by Ben, a full-time Dungeon Master. Players join recurring weekly campaigns with stable schedules, clear expectations, and a free Session Zero before committing to a paid seat.

How It Works

Three simple steps

  1. Pick a campaign or tell me what kind of game you want.
  2. Attend a free Session Zero.
  3. Reserve your weekly seat if the table feels right.

Austin D&D FAQ

Questions before you start

Do I need to live in Austin?

No. 20Fates is Austin-based, but online games are open to players who can consistently attend the listed Central Time schedule.

Are games online or in person?

Most 20Fates games run online through Discord + Roll20. That makes it easier for Austin-area players to keep a consistent weekly table without driving across town.

Do I need D&D experience?

No. Beginners are welcome. Session Zero and early sessions are built to make joining feel clear and supported.

What is Session Zero?

Session Zero is a free fit conversation where we talk through campaign tone, schedule, experience level, table expectations, and whether the table feels right.

How much does it cost?

Session Zero is free. Weekly campaign seats are $40/week after you decide the table is a good fit.

Can I join with friends?

Yes. Mention that on the Start form, and we can talk through seat availability, schedule fit, and whether the same table makes sense.

Ready to find your table?

Start with a free Session Zero. We will talk through the campaign, schedule, experience level, and whether the table feels right.
