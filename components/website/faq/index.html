Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need before Session Zero

Do I need experience to play?

No. New players are welcome. You do not need to buy rulebooks first, and Session Zero is designed to make the first step feel clear and low-pressure.

What kind of games do you run?

Player-driven, consequence-driven campaigns with consistent weekly momentum. The tone is welcoming and collaborative, with meaningful stakes and decisions that matter.

How does payment work?

Weekly campaign seats are billed at $40/week. Session Zero is always free and has no obligation to continue.

What happens if I miss a session?

Your seat remains reserved. Letting the DM know in advance helps with planning, but occasional absences are expected in long-running games.

What is Session Zero?

Session Zero is a free fit check where we cover campaign tone, scheduling, expectations, boundaries, and your questions before any weekly commitment.

How many players are at each table?

Most tables run around six players, with seats tracked by campaign so group dynamics stay stable over time.

Is this a safe and inclusive space?

Yes. Boundaries are respected, communication is taken seriously, and discrimination is not tolerated.

What platforms do you use?

Online sessions use Discord for voice and Roll20 for virtual tabletop tools.

Can I join with friends?

Yes. Mention that on the start form and we can discuss campaign fit and seat availability together.

Do you run solo campaigns?

Solo and private campaigns are available by inquiry based on schedule and fit.

Still have questions?

Start with Session Zero and we can talk through anything not covered here.
