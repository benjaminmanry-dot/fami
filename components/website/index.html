The Person Behind the Screen

I’m Ben. I’ll Help You Find Where You Fit.

Before you join anything, we’ll talk one-on-one about what you enjoy, how much D&D you’ve played, which nights work, and what helps you feel at home at a table.

If one of my campaigns is a good fit, I’ll tell you why. If it isn’t, I’ll tell you that too. I’m not trying to fill the nearest empty chair. I’m trying to help you find a weekly game you’ll still be excited to play six months from now.

I’ve been running tabletop games for more than 23 years. I run player-driven campaigns where choices matter, beginners get support, and everyone knows what to expect from the table.

I take the table seriously. I try not to take myself too seriously.

The Practical Part

A Real Weekly Table, with No Surprises.

Here’s what you’re signing up for—and what you aren’t.

The rhythm
One four-hour session every week, in a long-term campaign built to go somewhere.
The table
We play on Discord voice and Roll20. Beginners get support, and everyone gets clear expectations around boundaries, safety, and inclusion.
The rate
$10 per hour for a four-hour weekly session. Your ongoing seat stays reserved for you, including weeks you miss.
The first step
We talk first. Session Zero comes next. Both are free, and neither obligates you to join.
