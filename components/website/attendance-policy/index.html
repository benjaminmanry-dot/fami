Attendance Policy

Clear expectations for long-term campaigns

All campaigns run on a weekly schedule, and each player holds a reserved seat. When you join a campaign, you are committing to that shared rhythm.

Your weekly payment reserves your place in the table and supports campaign continuity, whether or not you attend a specific session.

Life happens. If you need to miss a week, let me know when you can. No personal details are required.

Occasional absences are expected in a long-running game. If absences become frequent, we check in and decide together on the best path forward for group momentum and fairness.

Major events such as health or family emergencies are handled case by case with communication and care.

Missing without communication still counts as that week's session. Repeated no-shows trigger a fit check conversation.

Quick summary

  • Campaign seats are billed weekly.
  • Payment reserves your seat.
  • Notice of absence is appreciated for planning.
  • Repeated extended absences prompt a check-in.
  • Communication always comes first.
