Table fit

The best crews can work a ballroom and a dungeon corridor.

No campaign is right for everyone. These are the clearest signals that Waterdeep and Undermountain will—or will not—feel like home.

You will love this if…

  • You want both city roleplay and deep dungeon progression.
  • You enjoy factions, secrets, and social leverage.
  • You like planning, resource management, and tactical teamwork.
  • You want one party continuity across two major campaign modes.

This might not be your table if…

  • You only want wilderness exploration.
  • You dislike extended dungeon environments.
  • You want disconnected one-shot adventures.
  • You prefer low-commitment drop-in scheduling.

Campaign FAQ

Is Dragon Heist separate from Dungeon of the Mad Mage?

No. This table treats them as one continuous campaign with the same party.

Is it all dungeon crawling?

No. The campaign begins with city intrigue and preserves social play even as Undermountain becomes central.

Can a newer player join the continuity?

Yes. Session Zero provides the context and onboarding needed.

How many seats are open?

One of seven seats is currently open.
