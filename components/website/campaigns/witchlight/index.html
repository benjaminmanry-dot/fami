Still at the midway

Three sessions in, the carnival is still winning.

The Witchlight Carnival rewards players who follow a strange detail, talk to everyone, and let curiosity set the pace. This table has spent its first three sessions doing exactly that. Table fit

The right party knows when a detour is the adventure.

No campaign is right for everyone. These are the clearest signals that Witchlight will—or will not—feel like home.

You will love this if…

  • You enjoy conversation, exploration, and character choices.
  • Joyful weirdness and fey wonder sound like a good game night.
  • You want the party’s curiosity to shape the pace.
  • You want beginner-friendly play with room to settle in.

This might not be your table if…

  • You want combat to drive every session.
  • You need the campaign to rush toward the next objective.
  • You dislike social exploration or playful detours.
  • You want grim realism without whimsy.

Campaign FAQ

Is the party stuck at the carnival?

No. The players are choosing the pace, and right now everyone is enjoying the carnival.

Has the group really barely rolled dice?

Yes. Conversation, curiosity, and character choices have carried most of the first three sessions.

Do I need to know the adventure before joining?

No. Session Zero gives you the context you need without assigning homework.

Is this beginner-friendly?

Yes. The current pace leaves plenty of room to learn through play.
