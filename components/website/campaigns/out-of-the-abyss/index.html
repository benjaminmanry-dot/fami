What Play Feels Like

This campaign rewards grit, teamwork, and creative survival under pressure. Every decision can change who makes it out.

You'll love this if...

  • You want tense survival challenges.
  • You enjoy hard-earned progress.
  • You like high stakes with real consequences.

This might not be your table if...

  • You want low-pressure, casual stakes.
  • You dislike scarcity and survival tension.
