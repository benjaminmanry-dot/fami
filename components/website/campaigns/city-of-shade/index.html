What Play Feels Like

City of Shade centers on conversation, leverage, hidden motives, and hard tradeoffs. You'll choose who to trust and what your character stands for when outcomes are uncertain.

You'll love this if...

  • You enjoy negotiation, alliances, and secrets.
  • You like social scenes with real consequences.
  • You want choices that reshape relationships.

This might not be your table if...

  • You only want combat-focused progression.
  • You dislike ambiguity in character motivations.
  • You prefer simple good-vs-evil framing.
