What Play Feels Like

Waterdeep is perfect for players who enjoy connecting dots, building plans, and taking calculated risks when the city pushes back.

You'll love this if...

  • You enjoy investigation and layered clues.
  • You like urban adventures with political tension.
  • You want problems with multiple solutions.

This might not be your table if...

  • You want only straightforward dungeon crawls.
  • You dislike gathering information before acting.
  • You prefer a single obvious path each session.
