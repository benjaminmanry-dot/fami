Your field briefing

Every nation wants leverage. Every city has something to hide.

Black Lanterns is a fast, sharp campaign of intrigue, field operations, and hard choices in Eberron’s political shadows.

Your team takes on sensitive jobs that mix investigation, infiltration, negotiation, and precision combat. Expect clear mission stakes and player-driven decisions that determine who gains power—and who disappears from the board.

Table fit

The right mission needs the right crew.

No campaign is right for everyone. These are the clearest signals that Black Lanterns will—or will not—feel like home.

You will love this if…

  • You enjoy mysteries, intelligence work, and layered conspiracies.
  • You want pulp action mixed with serious political stakes.
  • You prefer urban settings and mission-based structure.
  • You want guided onboarding without losing campaign depth.

This might not be your table if…

  • You only want wilderness exploration and dungeon focus.
  • You want low-pressure, consequence-light episodes.
  • You dislike social maneuvering or clue-based play.
  • You want a purely comedic tone every session.

No blind sign-up

Campaign FAQ

Do I need prior Eberron knowledge?

No. You will get the setting context needed to play confidently.

Is this more mystery or combat?

It is balanced, with mystery and social strategy driving where combat happens.

Are missions disconnected one-shots?

No. Jobs connect into a larger campaign arc with persistent consequences.

Can I play a character with a strong personal agenda?

Yes, and those agendas often create excellent campaign momentum.

Is this table good for beginners?

Yes. New players are welcome and onboarded with clear support.

Your next step

You do not need the perfect character. Just the right conversation.

Tell Ben what sounds exciting, what schedule works, and what would make a table feel comfortable. He will help you determine whether Black Lanterns is the right place to begin.
