The last line

The multiverse is ending unless you stop it.

A plane-hopping race against the arch-lich through D&D’s most iconic worlds, crossing paths with its most legendary figures.

Expect high-level play, maximum stakes, memorable set pieces, and choices whose consequences reach far beyond one kingdom or one plane. Preparation, trust, and decisive action determine whether your party becomes the line that holds.

Table fit

Impossible odds need a party that wants the responsibility.

No campaign is right for everyone. These are the clearest signals that Eve of Ruin will—or will not—feel like home.

You will love this if…

  • You want epic stakes and high-impact story beats.
  • You enjoy coordinated tactics in major encounters.
  • You like balancing heroism with difficult strategic choices.
  • You want to visit iconic worlds without losing party continuity.

This might not be your table if…

  • You seek low-stakes slice-of-life fantasy.
  • You prefer only slow, low-pressure sandbox pacing.
  • You dislike large narrative consequences.
  • You want disconnected one-shot progression.

Campaign FAQ

Is this too advanced for newer players?

New players are welcome. Session Zero and early sessions provide clear support.

Is the tone always grim?

The stakes are serious, but there is room for triumph, humor, and character-driven moments.

Do choices matter beyond combat?

Yes. Alliances and strategic decisions can change campaign outcomes.

Is this mostly combat?

Combat matters, but investigation, planning, and social decisions drive where it happens.
