Table fit

Long campaigns reward the player who wants to belong to one.

No campaign is right for everyone. These are the clearest signals that Epic Quests will—or will not—feel like home.

You will love this if…

  • You want long-term continuity and weekly commitment.
  • You enjoy deep party synergy and evolving consequences.
  • You value reliability and table culture as much as mechanics.
  • You want a campaign with a proven history rather than a disposable premise.

This might not be your table if…

  • None of the three weekly times can work for you.
  • You want casual drop-in scheduling.
  • You prefer low-commitment attendance patterns.
  • You only want short campaign arcs.
