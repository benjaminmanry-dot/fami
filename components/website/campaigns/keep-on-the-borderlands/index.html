What You'll Play

This is a high-agency frontier campaign built around scouting, planning, and hard-earned victories. The world does not wait for you, and smart play matters.

The Keep is your fragile line between civilization and chaos. Beyond it, rival factions, monster strongholds, and unstable alliances force the party to choose where to press, when to retreat, and what risks are worth taking. This table rewards teamwork, observation, and consequence-driven play where your decisions define what becomes safe, what becomes impossible, and what stories your group tells for years.

Campaign FAQ

Is this campaign mostly dungeon combat?

Combat matters, but scouting, alliances, logistics, and choosing where to push are equally important.

Can I join if I am newer to D&D?

Yes. New players are welcome, and Session Zero sets a clear foundation before play starts.

How punishing is the campaign?

Consequences are real, but fair. Smart decisions and teamwork are rewarded.

Do I need deep lore knowledge before joining?

No. You can join with zero setting knowledge and still succeed.

Is this more player-driven or DM-scripted?

Strongly player-driven. Your table decides priorities, alliances, and pacing.
