Table fit

The right traveler knows the strangest door is often the point.

No campaign is right for everyone. These are the clearest signals that Sigil will—or will not—feel like home.

You will love this if…

  • You enjoy strange settings and high-concept fantasy.
  • You want travel between radically different worlds.
  • You like faction choices, negotiation, and discovery.
  • You value character perspective as much as combat power.

This might not be your table if…

  • You want one grounded medieval region.
  • You dislike surreal locations or planar logic.
  • You want a purely linear dungeon campaign.
  • You need an immediate open seat.

Campaign FAQ

Is Sigil accepting players right now?

The table is currently 8/7. New interest goes to the waitlist.

Do I need Planescape lore?

No. The setting is introduced through play and Session Zero.

Does the campaign stay in Sigil?

Sigil is the crossroads. Its doors make travel to other worlds part of the campaign’s identity.

Can I ask about another table too?

Yes. Ben can discuss Sigil and currently open campaigns in the same conversation.
