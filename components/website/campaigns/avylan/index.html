Beyond the known map

Ancient systems outlived their meaning. They will not die quietly.

Avylan is an original setting you will not find in any book—and one that remembers what your party did to it.

Beneath a wounded moon, old powers continue long after their purpose has decayed. Exploration is player-driven, every discovery carries a cost, and your choices determine what the frontier becomes for everyone who follows.

Table fit

The frontier changes around the party willing to explore it.

No campaign is right for everyone. These are the clearest signals that Avylan will—or will not—feel like home.

You will love this if…

  • You want exploration with lasting consequences.
  • You enjoy discovering lore rather than receiving an assigned plot.
  • You want character goals to influence campaign direction.
  • You like a world where alliances and places remember your choices.

This might not be your table if…

  • You only want a familiar published setting.
  • You prefer a tightly scripted sequence of quests.
  • You dislike uncertainty or open-ended discovery.
  • You want a tightly guided path with every objective assigned.

Campaign FAQ

Is Avylan a sandbox or a story arc?

It is a living world with strong story momentum. Player priorities determine where that momentum goes.

Do I need setting lore?

No. The world is designed to be discovered through play.

Is this beginner-friendly?

Yes. New players receive clear onboarding and support.

Is the table accepting players now?

Yes. Two of seven seats are currently open.
