High magic, high ceremony, and negotiations with beings who have held grudges for ten thousand years.

Table fit

The right word can matter more than the sharpest sword.

No campaign is right for everyone. These are the clearest signals that Thrones will—or will not—feel like home.

You will love this if…

  • You enjoy political roleplay and strategic negotiation.
  • You want a high-magic setting with layered factions.
  • You like balancing ideals against practical survival.
  • You want combat whose stakes are shaped by relationships.

This might not be your table if…

  • You want nonstop dungeon crawling with little social play.
  • You prefer simple, binary moral conflicts.
  • You dislike managing relationships with powerful NPC factions.
  • You want a purely slapstick or low-tension tone.

Campaign FAQ

Is this campaign roleplay-heavy?

Yes, but tactical encounters remain a major part of the experience.

Do I need to be good at voices or acting?

No. Clear choices and collaboration matter more than performance style.

Will my character’s politics matter?

Absolutely. Beliefs, loyalties, and promises can reshape the campaign.

Is this beginner-friendly?

Yes. Session Zero and early play provide structure and guidance.

Your next step

You do not need to master court etiquette before you arrive.

Tell Ben what sounds exciting, what schedule works, and how much intrigue you want. He will help determine whether this court is the right place to begin.
