About the DM

23+ years running collaborative, consequence-driven campaigns

At this table, your choices matter, your voice is heard, and long-term story arcs are earned through play.

I started running tabletop roleplaying games more than twenty-three years ago. What kept me here was seeing people at their best around the table: creativity, humor, courage, and genuine collaboration.

My games are player-driven and consequence-driven. Not to punish mistakes, but to make decisions meaningful. I build a living world and let player choices shape what happens next.

New players are always welcome. You do not need to show up with rules mastery. Session Zero and early sessions are built to make the learning curve clear and manageable.

Safety and inclusion are non-negotiable. Boundaries are respected, communication is direct, and discrimination has no place at this table.

I run online with Discord and Roll20, and in-person tables in Austin as available.
